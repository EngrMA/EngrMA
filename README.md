## ⚡ About Me

My main research interests include:

* Power-system resilience and restoration
* Distribution-system optimization
* Stochastic and risk-aware optimization
* Electric vehicles and smart charging
* Renewable-energy forecasting
* Transportation–power-system coordination
* AI-assisted energy-management systems

---

## 🚀 Featured Work

### ⚡ Distribution-System Restoration

Transportation-aware and risk-based optimization for coordinating repair crews, mobile generators, EV aggregators, and critical-load restoration.

### ☀️ Renewable-Energy Forecasting

Machine-learning and deep-learning models for solar-power forecasting using historical generation and weather data.

### 🚗 EV Flexibility and Smart Charging

Forecasting and optimization of EV availability, charging flexibility, transformer loading, and grid-support services.

### 📊 Energy-System Dashboards

Interactive Streamlit dashboards for power-system monitoring, optimization, visualization, and engineering decision support.
